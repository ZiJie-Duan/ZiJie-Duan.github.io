## Common questions

### Utter isn't in my keyboard list

Open iOS Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard and choose Utter. Then tap or hold the globe key while typing to switch to it.

### Voice doesn't respond, or asks me to wake it

- Make sure Allow Full Access is on for Utter in the keyboard settings.
- Open the Utter app and check that Voice is on. iOS does not allow starting the microphone in the background, so after standby ends you need to wake it once from the app.

### The text wasn't tidied up, or I see no key points

In the Utter app's Settings, under AI Cleanup, check that "Tidy Up Dictation" and "Show Key Points While Talking" are on, and that you are online. On a poor connection Utter makes sure your words arrive in full first and may skip the tidying.

### The app crashed or the keyboard closed mid-sentence. Is my dictation lost?

No. Recordings are saved on your device as you talk. Next time you open the keyboard, "Continue recording" appears at the top; tap it to finish and have everything typed in.

### How do I delete my data?

Deleting the Utter app deletes everything it keeps on your device. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/) for more.
