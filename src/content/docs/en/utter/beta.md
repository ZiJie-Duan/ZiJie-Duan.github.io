Thank you for testing Utter. Utter is a voice-first keyboard for Chinese: tap to talk, tap again to finish, and tidy text is typed into whatever you are using. While you talk, the keyboard lists the key points you have covered.

## You need

- An iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
- The [TestFlight](https://apps.apple.com/app/testflight/id899247664) app from the App Store.
- An internet connection for voice.

## Install and set up

1. On your iPhone, open the TestFlight invitation link you were sent (or the button above) and install Utter in TestFlight.
2. Open iOS Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard, and choose **Utter**.
3. Tap Utter in the list and turn on **Allow Full Access**. Voice input needs it; what you type is still not uploaded (see the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/)).
4. Open the Utter app, allow the microphone, follow the voice setup and turn Voice on.
5. In any text field, switch to Utter with the globe key, tap Voice to start talking and tap it again to finish.

## Please try

- **Pinyin typing**: are the candidates still right when your taps are a little off, or one or two keys are wrong?
- **Voice**: is recognition accurate, are fillers, repeats and self-corrections cleaned up, do punctuation and paragraphs feel natural?
- **Key points**: talk for a while; do the points sum up well and keep up?
- **Reliability**: after a bad connection, a call, or closing the keyboard mid-sentence, does "Continue recording" appear at the top with everything intact?
- **Speed**: opening the keyboard, starting to talk, text appearing after you finish: any stutter or wait?

## Sending feedback

- **Easiest**: take a screenshot, tap its thumbnail, choose "Share Beta Feedback" and describe what happened.
- Or open Utter in TestFlight and tap "Send Beta Feedback".
- Or email [{{email}}](mailto:{{email}}).

Please say which app's text field you were in, what you did, what you expected and what happened.

## Known limitations

- iOS does not let apps start the microphone in the background. With Voice on, Utter stays ready; if iOS ends that (for example after a restart), you will be taken to Utter once to wake it.
- Calls, Siri or other apps using the microphone pause voice; it resumes when they finish.
- Betas update often; the interface and features may change.

## Privacy

Typing is handled on your device and never uploaded. For voice, the recording and a little related text are sent to Alibaba Cloud Model Studio for recognition and tidying. See the full [Privacy Policy](../privacy/).
